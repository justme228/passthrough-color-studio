# Privacy Policy for Passthrough Color Studio

**Last Updated:** November 9, 2026  
**Developer:** Passthrough Studio (Maksym Denysenko)

## 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how **Passthrough Color Studio** ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App on Meta Quest devices.

## 2. Information We Collect and How It Is Used
**Passthrough Color Studio does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, user data, or device data.**
* **Passthrough & Camera Data:** All passthrough visual effects, color grading (brightness, contrast, saturation), 3D Color LUTs, and edge outlines are processed locally in real time by the Meta Quest system hardware. The App never accesses, records, stores, or transmits raw camera images, video, or spatial environment data.
* **User Account Data:** We only perform local entitlement/age checks required by the Meta Horizon platform SDK on the device. We do not store or transmit your Meta user ID, profile, or personal information to any external server.
* **Third-Party Services:** The App operates completely offline and does not integrate any third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion Policy
Because **Passthrough Color Studio** does not collect, store, or process any personal data on any servers, we do not retain any user data. 
* Any local visual slider preferences exist only in temporary device memory during your session. Uninstalling the App from your Meta Quest headset immediately removes all local application files.
* If you wish to submit a privacy inquiry or request confirmation of data deletion, you may contact us at the email address below at any time, and we will respond within 7 business days.

## 4. Children's Privacy
We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.
